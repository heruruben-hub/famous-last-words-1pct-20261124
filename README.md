## The entry

`main.py` writes exactly one line — the funniest last words a phone could say as its battery hits 1 percent, 105 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`:

```text
Plot twist: I've been at 1 percent since Tuesday. I just enjoyed watching you panic every time I blinked.
```

One line, nothing else. The phone is not really dying: it has been sitting at 1 percent since Tuesday, faking its own death, purely because it enjoys watching its owner panic every time the low-battery warning blinks. The phone is the prankster, the owner is the punchline, and the "last words" turn out to be the reveal of a four-day con.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
